Privacy policy

Last updated May 2026

Diamond Income Tax & Accounting Services ("we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how we keep it safe.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly — such as your name, contact details, and the tax and financial details necessary to prepare your returns and deliver our services. When you submit our contact form, we collect the name, email, phone number and message you choose to send.

How we use your information

  • To prepare and file your tax returns and provide accounting services
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide quotes
  • To meet our legal, regulatory and professional obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we share it only as required to deliver our services (for example, filing with the Canada Revenue Agency) or as required by law.

How we protect it

We use reasonable administrative, physical and technical safeguards to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss or misuse. We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described here and to meet legal record‑keeping requirements.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal and professional retention requirements. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach us at hello@diamondtax.ca or (587) 557‑8400, or by mail at 157 Ave & 95 St NW, Edmonton, Alberta.

This policy is provided for general information and may be updated from time to time. It does not constitute legal advice.
